# Email quarantine release policy (2026 revision)

Supersedes the 2024 revision. This is the policy in force.

## Release authority
A user may release a message quarantined as spam. A message quarantined as phishing or as
malware may be released only by the Security Team.

## Hold period
Quarantined messages are held for 30 days and then deleted. A released message is
delivered to the mailbox within 10 minutes.

## Reporting
A user who believes a quarantine decision was wrong reports it in the mail console. The
report goes to the Security Team queue and is answered within 1 business day.

## Bulk release
Bulk release is available to the Security Team only and is recorded against the operator.
