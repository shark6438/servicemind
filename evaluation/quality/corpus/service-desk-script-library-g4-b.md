# Service Desk shift handover rules

Restricted to the Service Desk. Not for general distribution.

## Handover
A handover is held at 08:00 and 16:00, and covers every open SEV1 and SEV2 ticket.

## Content
Each ticket is handed over with its current state, the next action, and the time of the
next promised contact.

## Priority tickets
A ticket awaiting a customer response for more than 48 hours is chased once and then
lowered in priority.

## Recording
The handover is recorded in the Service Desk channel.
