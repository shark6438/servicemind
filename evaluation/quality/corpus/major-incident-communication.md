# Major incident communication

Applies to SEV1 and SEV2 incidents. Owned by the Service Management Office.

## Cadence
The first customer-facing notice is published within 30 minutes of declaration. Updates
follow every 30 minutes for SEV1 and hourly for SEV2 until the incident is resolved.

## Content
A notice states the affected service, the observed impact, and the time of the next
update. A notice never states a cause that has not been confirmed, and never gives a
recovery time the incident commander has not committed to.

## Audience
The customer notice is published to the status page. Internal stakeholders receive the
same text plus the incident channel link. Regulators are notified only where the incident
involves personal data, and that decision belongs to the Data Protection Officer.

## After resolution
A resolution notice is published within 30 minutes of the incident being resolved.
