# Service Desk call script library

Restricted to the Service Desk. Not for general distribution.

## Greeting
The greeting states the operator's name, the team name, and the ticket reference.

## Verification
The caller is verified against the employee directory record. A caller who cannot be
verified is offered a callback, and the ticket records the failed verification.

## Handling an angry caller
The operator acknowledges the impact, states what will happen next, and gives a time for
the next contact. The operator does not speculate about cause.

## Closing
The ticket is closed only after the caller confirms the issue is resolved, or after 3
business days with no response.
