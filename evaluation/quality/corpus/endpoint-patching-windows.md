# Endpoint patching windows

Applies to managed laptops and servers. Owned by the Endpoint Team.

## Windows
Servers patch on the second Saturday of the month between 02:00 and 06:00. Laptops patch
within 72 hours of a patch becoming available, and defer up to 3 times.

## Critical patches
A patch rated critical by the vendor is deployed within 14 days of release regardless of
the monthly window. The 14 day clock starts at vendor release, not at internal triage.

## Reboot
A server reboot is scheduled with the owning team. A laptop reboot is user-initiated
within the 72 hour window; after the window the reboot is enforced.

## Exceptions
An exception needs the service owner and expires after 90 days. An expired exception
returns the endpoint to the standard policy without a further decision.
