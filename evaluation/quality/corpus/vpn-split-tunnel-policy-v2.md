# VPN split tunnel policy (revision 2)

Supersedes revision 1. This is the policy in force.

## Routing
Only corporate subnets are routed through the tunnel. General internet traffic leaves by
the local path.

## Which subnets
The routed subnets are published in the access portal and refreshed daily.

## Exceptions
A full-tunnel profile is available on request for staff handling regulated data. It needs
the Security Team's agreement and is reviewed every 6 months.

## Monitoring
Traffic leaving by the local path is not inspected. Traffic inside the tunnel is.
