# On-call rotation handbook (revision 2)

Supersedes revision 1. This is the handbook in force.

## Rotation
The rotation is one week, handing over Monday at 10:00. A shift is never extended beyond
two consecutive weeks.

## Handover
A handover covers open incidents, pending changes, and any known degraded state. It is
recorded in the on-call channel.

## Escalation
If the primary does not acknowledge a page within 10 minutes, the secondary is paged. If
neither acknowledges within 20 minutes, the engineering manager is paged.

## Compensation
Time worked outside business hours is recorded and taken as time off in the following
month.
