# IP address management conventions

Restricted to the Network Team. Not for general distribution.

## Allocation
Addresses are allocated from the management system, never by hand. A manual allocation is
recorded in the management system within 1 business day.

## Ranges
The `10.40.0.0/16` range is reserved for the satellite offices. `10.41.0.0/16` is reserved
for lab use and is not routed.

## Reservations
A reservation for a decommissioned device is released within 5 business days.

## Audit
The address management system is reconciled against the DHCP leases monthly.
