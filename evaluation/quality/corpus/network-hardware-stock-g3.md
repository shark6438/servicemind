# Network spare hardware stock levels

Restricted to the Network Team. Not for general distribution.

## Stock
The Network Team holds 4 spare access switches, 2 spare distribution switches, and 10
spare transceivers.

## Reorder point
A reorder is raised when spares fall below half the listed stock.

## Location
Spares are held in the data hall cage, bay 3. Access is by the Network Team badge group.

## Ageing
A spare held more than 3 years without use is returned to the vendor for credit.
