# Backup retention schedule (revision 3)

This revision has been withdrawn. It is retained for reference only.

## Retention
Daily backups are kept 7 days. Weekly backups are kept 4 weeks. Monthly backups are kept
12 months.

## Deletion
Deletion is performed by an operator each month.

## Offsite
A copy is written offsite weekly.

## Restore requests
A restore request is answered within 1 business day.
