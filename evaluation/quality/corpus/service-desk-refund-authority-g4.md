# Service Desk refund authority

Restricted to the Service Desk. Not for general distribution.

## Authority
A Service Desk operator may authorise a refund up to 100 units without a further
approval.

## Above the limit
A refund above 100 units needs the Service Desk team lead. Above 1000 units needs the
service owner.

## Evidence
A refund is recorded with the ticket reference and the reason.

## Monthly report
Refunds are reported monthly by reason code.
