# Incident severity matrix

Applies to all production incidents. Owned by the Service Management Office.

## Levels
SEV1 is a total loss of a customer-facing service, or any loss of confidentiality.
SEV2 is a degraded customer-facing service affecting more than 25 percent of requests.
SEV3 is a degraded internal service, or a customer-facing degradation under 25 percent.
SEV4 is a fault with no service impact.

## Response times
SEV1 acknowledges within 5 minutes and updates every 30 minutes. SEV2 acknowledges within
15 minutes and updates hourly. SEV3 acknowledges within 4 hours. SEV4 is handled in
business hours.

## Review
A SEV1 requires a review within 5 business days. A SEV2 requires a review within 10
business days. A SEV3 is reviewed at the monthly service meeting.
