# Password reset procedure

Applies to staff and contractor accounts. Owned by the Identity Team.

## Self service
Self-service reset requires a second factor. The reset link expires 15 minutes after it
is issued and is single use.

## Assisted reset
An assisted reset is performed by the Service Desk on the identity of the caller. The
operator must verify the caller against the employee directory record, not against
information supplied by the caller. The new password is delivered out of band.

## Policy
Minimum length is 16 characters. Rotation is not required on a schedule; rotation is
required on evidence of compromise. The previous 24 passwords cannot be reused.

## Lockout
Five failed attempts lock the account for 30 minutes. A lockout clears automatically and
an operator cannot clear it early.
