# Print service quota policy (revision 2)

Supersedes revision 1. This is the policy in force.

## Quota
Each account receives 500 pages per calendar month, pooled across all printers.

## Overdraft
A 100 page overdraft is allowed. Beyond the overdraft, printing is refused until the next
month.

## Charging
Pages are charged to the cost centre recorded on the account. A page printed in duplex
counts as one page.

## Exceptions
An exception needs the cost centre owner and is granted for one month at a time.
