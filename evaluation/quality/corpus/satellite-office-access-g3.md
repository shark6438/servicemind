# Satellite office physical access rules

Restricted to the Network Team and the Facilities liaison. Not for general distribution.

## Access
The satellite office server room requires a badge with the `dc-satellite` group and a
second person present.

## Visitors
A visitor is escorted at all times and signs the room log.

## Keys
Physical keys are held in the Facilities safe. A key issued to an engineer is returned
within 24 hours of the visit.

## Alarm
The alarm code is changed quarterly and after any leaver with access.
