# Change approval and the change advisory board

Applies to all production changes. Owned by the Service Management Office.

## Categories
A standard change is pre-approved and needs no board. A normal change needs one approver
from the owning team. A significant change needs the change advisory board.

## Board
The board meets every Tuesday and Thursday. Papers close 24 hours before the meeting. A
significant change not on the agenda is not approved at the meeting.

## Emergency change
An emergency change needs the incident commander and one board member, and is reviewed at
the next scheduled board. Emergency approval does not waive the review.

## Lead time
A significant change needs 5 business days between approval and implementation.
