# Laptop disk encryption standard (revision 3)

Supersedes revision 2. This is the standard in force.

## Requirement
Every managed laptop encrypts its system volume with the approved full-disk encryption
product. FileVault or BitLocker are the approved products.

## Key escrow
The recovery key is escrowed automatically at enrolment. A recovery key is never held by
the user alone.

## Verification
Compliance is reported daily. A laptop out of compliance for 7 days is blocked from the
corporate network, and an out-of-compliance laptop is never granted a permanent exemption.

## Replacement
A replacement laptop is encrypted before it is issued. Data is migrated by the Endpoint
Team, not by copying the disk.
