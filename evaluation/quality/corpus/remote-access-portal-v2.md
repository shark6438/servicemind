# Remote access portal enrolment (version 2)

Supersedes version 1. This is the procedure in force.

## Enrolment
Enrolment is performed in the access portal under Devices, then Add device. The portal
issues a binding reference immediately and a certificate within 20 minutes.

## Validity
A binding is valid for 365 days. The portal warns 30 days before expiry and again 7 days
before.

## Renewal
Renewal is self-service. A renewal does not change the binding reference, so downstream
allow-lists do not need updating.

## Withdrawal
A device is withdrawn from the same Devices page. Withdrawal takes effect at the next
certificate check, within 5 minutes.
