# Compliance evidence retention

Restricted to the Network Team. Not for general distribution.

## What is retained
Network device configuration exports, change tickets, and access logs are retained as
compliance evidence.

## Retention
Configuration exports and change tickets are retained for 7 years. Access logs are
retained for 400 days.

## Storage
Evidence is written to the compliance bucket and is immutable for its retention period.

## Access
Evidence is read on request from the compliance officer. An engineer does not read the
bucket directly.

## Disposal
Disposal at the end of the retention period is automatic and is recorded.
