# Data hall power maintenance procedure

Restricted to the Network Team. Not for general distribution.

## Planned work
Planned power work requires 10 business days notice in the maintenance calendar.

## Load
Before any feed is isolated, the remaining feed is confirmed below 60 percent load.

## Supervision
Power work is performed by two engineers, one of whom is a qualified electrical
authorised person.

## Abort
Work is aborted if the remaining feed exceeds 70 percent load at any point.
