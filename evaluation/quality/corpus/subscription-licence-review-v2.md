# Software subscription licence review (revision 2)

Supersedes revision 1. This is the process in force.

## Cadence
Licences are reviewed every quarter, in the first month of the quarter.

## Reclamation
A licence unused for 60 days is reclaimed. The holder is notified 14 days before
reclamation and may object once.

## Reporting
Utilisation is reported per vendor. A vendor above 95 percent utilisation is flagged for
an early renewal conversation.

## Renewal
Renewal is agreed 60 days before expiry. An auto-renewing subscription is cancelled no
later than 90 days before expiry.
