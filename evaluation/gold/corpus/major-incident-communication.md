# Major Incident Communication Playbook

## Trigger

A major incident is declared when a P1 fault affects a business-critical service and
the recovery effort needs a dedicated bridge.

## Communication steps

1. Open the incident bridge and post the bridge link in the #major-incident channel.
2. Appoint a single incident commander; all status posts come from that role.
3. Publish an initial update within fifteen minutes of declaration.
4. Post updates every thirty minutes until recovery, then a final post-mortem link.

## Update contents

Each update states current impact, the owning team, the suspected cause, and the
next action. Updates never promise a specific fix time.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Incident commander | Decisions, single voice |
| Scribe | Timeline and decision log |
| Communications | Customer and executive updates |

## After the bridge

Recovery is not the end. The commander hands a complete timeline to the change
process so the fix becomes a tracked change, and the review captures lessons.
