# VPN MFA Operations Note

## Scope

Operational guidance from the VPN operations rota. Same authority as the token store
procedure; written by a different team.

## Reissue interval

The operations rota works to a reissue interval of 72 hours. Reissuing more often than that
is treated as a change to the identity store and is expected to be approved by the change
advisory board.

## Rationale

Bulk reissues were the largest single source of sign-in incidents last quarter, and the
rota's view is that a slower interval absorbs them.

## Open question

This note and the token store procedure give different intervals. Neither team has
reconciled them.
